Slide 4 · Open access means anyone can read your paper, by one of four routes

1.5 min · by the end 4.5 min · can skip

Say
Open access means free to read online. Gold is the journal opening it, often with an article processing charge. Green is you placing a version in a repository. Diamond charges neither reader nor author. Hybrid is a subscription journal that opens one paper for a fee.
Next
Now, how do you find the right journal among thousands?
Source
Budapest Open Access Initiative (2002). Action Plan for Diamond Open Access (2022).

APC means article processing charge. Mention that the route changes the cost, so the journal choice is also a budget choice.

Say
Think of it as matchmaking by vocabulary. Elsevier's JournalFinder builds a fingerprint of weighted terms from your abstract and compares it with published articles. JANE compares your text with millions of PubMed records. A journal that has published papers like yours ranks high.

Slide 11 · A finder only suggests, so verify four things before you submit

Slide 12 · In India, journal checks moved from one central list to each institution

1 min · by the end 16 min · can skip

Say
A 2018 study from SPPU, Pune, found most journals recommended for the UGC list were of poor quality. UGC removed 4,305 journals that year and built the CARE list in 2019, screened at SPPU. In 2025 UGC discontinued the list. Each institution now judges journals using suggested parameters.

Likely questions

Which finder should a social science or humanities scholar use?

JANE covers PubMed, so it suits biomedical work. Use the publisher finders (Elsevier, Springer Nature) for other fields, and cross-check in Scopus or Web of Science and DOAJ.

Is a journal with an APC predatory?

No. Many genuine gold open access journals charge an APC. Predatory journals charge without real peer review. Check DOAJ and the database indexing, not the fee alone.

Is Sherpa Romeo still available?

Jisc combined Sherpa Romeo, Juliet and Fact into Open Policy Finder in 2024. It shows what version you may self-archive and when.

Since the UGC-CARE list is gone, how do we judge a journal?

UGC asked institutions to set up their own evaluation using its suggested parameters, such as editorial board, peer review policy, ethics and visibility. Check your university's current rule.

Do journal finders keep my abstract private?

Each tool has its own terms. Do not paste unpublished results you are not ready to share. Read the tool's privacy note first.

Should I just choose the highest Impact Factor?

No. Scope fit decides acceptance. A good fit in a solid indexed journal beats a desk rejection from a famous one.

What was the SPPU tool?

SPPU's Centre for Publication Ethics ran the screening protocol behind the UGC-CARE list. Its current status should be checked, since the CARE list was discontinued in 2025.
