    Open access means anyone can read your paper, by one of four routes

    GoldJournal opens itFree to read, may charge an APC
    GreenYou archive itA version in a repository, often after an embargo
    DiamondFree both waysNo fee to read, no fee to publish
    HybridPay to openA subscription journal opens one article for a fee

    Source: Budapest Open Access Initiative (2002). Action Plan for Diamond Open Access (2022).

      Question 3 of 4 · scored

      JANE shows a tag beside a suggested journal. Which tag should raise your trust?

      1. Indexed in MEDLINE
      2. Publication in 7 days
      3. Invited by email
      4. Lowest fee

      A finder only suggests, so verify four things before you submit

      IndexingSearch the database itselfScopus or Web of Science, not the journal's own claim
      Open accessDOAJLists vetted open access journals
      RightsOpen Policy FinderFormerly Sherpa Romeo: what you may self-archive, and when
      ChecklistThink. Check. Submit.A short checklist to judge a journal

      Source: Jisc, Open Policy Finder (2024). doaj.org. thinkchecksubmit.org.

      In India, journal checks moved from one central list to each institution

      1. 2018SPPU study: about 88% of journals recommended for the UGC list were poor
      2. May 2018UGC removes 4,305 journals from its list
      3. 2019UGC-CARE list, screened at SPPU’s Centre for Publication Ethics
      4. 2025CARE list discontinued, institutions now evaluate journals

      Source: Nature Index (3 May 2018). Scholarly Kitchen (5 Feb 2020). UGC 584th meeting (3 Oct 2024), announced Feb 2025.

      Question 4 of 4 · scored

      An email promises review in 7 days, an Impact Factor of 9.8 from an unknown index, and a low fee. Your move?

      1. Submit, it is fast and cheap
      2. Check Scopus or Web of Science, DOAJ and Think. Check. Submit. first
      3. Ask them for a discount
      4. Submit there and to another journal at once
