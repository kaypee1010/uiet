Publishers agree: an AI tool may help you write, but it cannot be an author

COPE position statement · Feb 2023
  • "AI tools cannot be listed as an author of a paper" because they "cannot take responsibility for the submitted work".
  • Disclose in Methods (or similar) which tool and how it was used.
  • "Authors are fully responsible for the content of their manuscript, even those parts produced by an AI tool."
ICMJE recommendations
  • Chatbots "should not be listed as authors because they cannot be responsible for the accuracy, integrity, and originality of the work".
  • Describe the use in both the cover letter and the submitted work.
  • "Nondisclosure of AI use … may be construed as misconduct in some circumstances."

Sources: COPE, "Authorship and AI tools", position statement, 13 Feb 2023; ICMJE Recommendations, "AI use by authors" (current text).

The line is not "used AI or not"; it is disclosure, accountability and fabrication

Where guidance places each use of AI
UseWhere the guidance puts it
A · Grammar and spellingfine; disclose if a tool rewrote substantial text
B · Summaries for your own notesfine; verify before you cite anything from it
C · Writing the discussionallowed only with disclosure; you remain responsible for every claim (COPE, ICMJE)
D · Generating a figurefabrication unless clearly labelled as an illustration; a figure is data (the Frontiers case)
E · Generating the datafabrication, Unit 2.3; no disclosure repairs it

Blind spot: plagiarism software would score all five at 0% similarity. The UGC 2018 levels (10%, 40%, 60%) never see any of this.

Sources: COPE position statement (2023); ICMJE Recommendations; UGC (Promotion of Academic Integrity and Prevention of Plagiarism in HEIs) Regulations, 2018; Frontiers statement, 16 Feb 2024.

Three rules you can apply tomorrow

Disclose the tool and what it didName it in Methods and the cover letter. Silence is the misconduct, not the tool.
Never let it make data, figures or referencesGenerated data is fabrication. Check that every suggested citation exists.
You sign, you answerRead every sentence as if a reviewer will ask you about it. One will.
